Three roles

One platform. Every role.

Purpose-built tools for owners, coaches, and athletes — each with their own view, their own controls, and their own lane.

👑

Owner Dashboard

Full gym visibility — member stats, revenue MTD, leaderboard snapshot, WOD programming, check-in, membership management, and one-click data export. Everything that matters in one screen.

🎽

Coach Tools

Program WODs with 14 scoring types, run check-in with walk-in support, manage personal training services, add private athlete notes, and message your athletes directly.

🏋️

Athlete Experience

View the schedule, register for classes, log scores, track PRs, see live leaderboard standings, book personal training sessions, and engage with the gym community.

Full feature set

Everything a gym actually needs.

Built inside a real CrossFit affiliate — Wolverine Strength & Conditioning in Ann Arbor — and refined with coaches and athletes testing every feature.

💪

WOD Programming

14 scoring types including time, weight, rounds+reps, EMOM, Hyrox, and more. Multi-section WODs, movement tracking, and coach notes pulled from the last session.

🏆

Live Leaderboard

Real-time class scores by Rx level. Daily and all-time rankings. Emoji reactions and comments on every score. Community engagement built directly into the platform.

🎯

Personal Training

Coaches list services with pricing, duration, and booking mode. Athletes browse and book in seconds. Instant or approval-based — coaches stay in control.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Week, month, and quarter views. Class capacity tracking. WOD assignment directly from the schedule. Athletes can register and see who else is in class.

💳

Memberships

Create unlimited, per-week, per-month, and punch card membership types. Review and approve applications. Track expiring memberships across your roster.

🛍️

Gym Store

Members purchase memberships, book personal training, and buy merch — all through one app. No third-party logins, no extra friction, no missed revenue.
